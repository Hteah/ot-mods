Two-level steps, as on a TR-909: each sample trig is FULL or SOFT, and the
trig LED shows which (bright red or dim red).

## Access

In GRID RECORDING on an audio track, tap a trig key: an empty step becomes
a FULL trig, a FULL trig becomes SOFT, a SOFT trig is removed. Not in an
official remix; build the test remix `accent`.

Hold a TRACK key and tap CUE to switch ACCENT off or on (a toast says
which). It is on after every power-up. CUE held first, then TRACK, cues as
stock.

## Operation

- SOFT is an AMP VOL lock at the track's Part AMP VOL less 6 dB (64 → 45),
  so it saves, copies and pastes like any lock and shows in the LOCK view.
- Any trig locked quieter than the track's Part AMP VOL lights dim.
- The lock is a fixed value: after turning the Part's AMP VOL, re-tap soft
  steps to follow it.
- Holding a trig to edit its locks works as stock; the release keeps the
  trig as it was.
- MIDI tracks are stock.
